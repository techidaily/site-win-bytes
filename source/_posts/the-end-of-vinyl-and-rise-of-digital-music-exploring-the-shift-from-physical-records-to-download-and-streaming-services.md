## The End of Vinyl & Rise of Digital Music: Exploring the Shift From Physical Records to Download and Streaming Services

**John Young** from [_Disc Jockey News_](http://www.discjockeynews.com/) gives us a brief video report from the [_Digital Entertainment World_](http://www.dewexpo.com/) conference in Los Angeles CA on the state of the digital music industry. 

_**"Vinyl is Dead"**_ 

 John reports that there was virtually _no discussion_ regarding the suggestion that vinyl is making a comeback (not a shocker), and that **pay-per-downloads** of music are also going the way of the caveman.   **Streaming is the talk of the conference, and the future of the industry as a whole.** 

 It seems the perception that people want to own what they pay for is an old-school mentality. Rent to use is indeed the future. Let's hope some of the big players such as Spotify secure commercial licensing so DJ's (and DJ software such as ourselves) can implement professional subscription solutions.
