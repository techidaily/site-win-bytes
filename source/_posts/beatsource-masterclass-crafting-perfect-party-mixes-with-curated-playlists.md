## BeatSource Masterclass: Crafting Perfect Party Mixes with Curated Playlists

[![Beatsource LINK Curated Playlists](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/beatsourcelink.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/beatsourcelink.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1 "Beatsource LINK Curated Playlists")

[Beatsource LINK](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is baked into [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), providing subscribers to the streaming service with access to millions of tracks, including DJ-friendly edits, from within the file browser.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Beatsource LINK in DEX 3](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/playlist-to-dex3.png?fit=300%2C213&ssl=1 "Beatsource LINK in DEX 3")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/playlist-to-dex3.png?fit=1030%2C730&ssl=1)

While you can search their massive catalog on-the-fly from within DEX 3, an underrepresented feature of the record-pool-gone-streaming platform is curated playlists.

> _Dig through hundreds of themed playlists professionally curated by our network of world-class DJs, or create your own using tracks from our huge library of hits. – **Beatsource LINK**_

If you’re brand new to DJing or have been booked for an event where you’re not familiar with the genre of music you’ll need to perform with, the curated playlist feature is big boon.

For example, check out this [Wedding Dinner – Modern Pop playlist](https://www.beatsource.com/playlist/wedding-dinner-modern-pop/1427). If you wish to add it to DEX 3, nested under the “Beatsource” logo in the file browser, just click “Save To Library”. Refresh Beatsource in DEX 3 (right click on Beatsource and select “Refresh Playlists”) and boom – the playlist will appear automagically.

Beatsource LINK allows you to DJ any type of event with their expansive catalog of open-format music. Whether it’s a house party, festival, corporate gig or wedding, you’re covered.

If you perform at an event without an available internet connection, you can download up to 100 tracks for offline playback.

If you haven’t already, [try Beatsource LINK free for 30 days](https://www.beatsource.com/subscriptions/billing?plan=bsrc%5Flink%5Fpro%5Fplus) (3 tiers available). It’s your LINK to open-format music in [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)!
