We hear about it all the time via our support ticketing system and conversations with karaoke hosts. Many thousands of karaoke files in their library — no standard file naming convention. When importing karaoke songs into [karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or when attempting to [create a karaoke song book](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) it can be a overly tedious time-consuming task because in the karaoke world, there isn’t one standard way karaoke files are named.

The **karaoke file naming convention** (file name structure) can vary greatly depending on where you bought the karaoke song, or if you converting a CDG disc to MP3+G (or zip) and/or acquiring your karaoke library by other means. Thus your karaoke library can get messy quickly, making all the other related tasks (such as creating a [karaoke song book](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)) a real pain.

For example, you can batch rename all your karaoke music to: “TrackID – Artist – Title”.

When you have a continuity among your **karaoke file names** it makes all other tasks, such as importing your karaoke files into [karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) or creating [karaoke song books](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), a much simpler task.
