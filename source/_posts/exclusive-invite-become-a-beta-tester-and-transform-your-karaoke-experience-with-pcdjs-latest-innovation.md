## Karaoki Closed Beta-Test Requirements

* A current owner of PCDJ Karaoki
* You must own a computer that meets the minimum Karaoki system requirements:

#### System Requirements

**WINDOWS**

* Windows XP / Windows Vista / Windows 7 / Windows 8 (8.1) / Windows 10
* Intel “I” series 3 or greater or AMD 64 x 2
* DirectX compatible sound card (Standard in Windows)
* 4 GIG RAM or Better
* 200 MB free on the hard-drive
* Graphics Card (ATI / Nvidia) with 256MB minimum dedicated graphics RAM,  
or Intel HD 4000 series shared graphics cards (or higher version number)
* Internet access for Karaoki at the venue (_or location_) you will be testing the new features at.
* You must be willing to sign a basic N.D.A. ([Non-Disclosure Agreement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure%5Fagreement)) — and can sign, scan and email it back.
* You will be required to report on any issues and/or usability concerns. Anyone that doesn’t actively participate in reporting will be removed from the beta team.

#### Bonus Preference

* Karaoke Cloud Pro subscription subscriber

If you meet these requirements please submit the form below. A PCDJ production team member will get back to you. (**NOTE:** _Not all testers will be accepted as we have limited space on the BETA testing team_)
